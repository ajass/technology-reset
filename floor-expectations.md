## What Changes (the autonomy shift)

- We stop actively timing gaming sessions during free time
- We step back from routine monitoring of conversations (safety protections stay)
- They get more say in how they spend free time, within the floor
- We shift from "enforcing screen limits" to "checking in on how things are going"

---

## Consequences

**In the moment:**
- If on screens and the floor isn't met, we redirect. Get it done, then screens are back.
- Comply (even grudgingly) = Yellow on the tracker. Refuse = Red.

**Pattern (3+ Reds in a week):**
- We sit down with the tracker data. The data shows this isn't working yet.
- Structural rollback: Aaron or Denise verifies homework and chores before screens open. For 2 weeks.
- After 2 weeks, we re-test autonomy.

**There's always a path back to autonomy.** Show us you can handle the structure, and the freedom comes back.
