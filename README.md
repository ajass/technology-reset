# Technology Reset — Floor Expectations

**Aaron & Denise | November 2026**

## Executive Summary

Sean and Liam are 11-12 years old. We've been managing their screen time closely, and it's not working anymore — they spend their screen-free time waiting to get back on devices, and we spend our energy enforcing restrictions. This is a structured approach to shifting from **managing their screen time** to **helping them manage their own time**, within a clear responsibility floor.

### The Approach

1. **Build the floor first, then test autonomy.** Responsibilities (homework, chores, family participation) are the floor — they happen regardless. Screens are the discretionary time that comes after. We put the floor in place before we test screen autonomy, not at the same time.

2. **30-day trial with data.** We track four categories daily (Homework, Chores, Bedtime, Screen Transition) using a shared Google Sheet. Baseline data first, then trial data. At 30 days, the data does the talking — not gut feeling.

3. **Structural rollback, not punishment.** If the tracker shows 3+ Reds in a week, we roll back to parent-checked structure for 2 weeks, then re-test autonomy. Always a path back to freedom.

### Key Decisions

- **Homework first, not time-based.** Responsibilities before screens, no clock to fight over.
- **Screens off at 9pm = ALL screens** (including reels). No co-watching exceptions.
- **No screens during dinner.** The one hard boundary during free time.
- **Game-by-game evaluation.** Not platform bans — we approve individual titles.
- **Weekly chores on Saturday.** Sunday is a spot check.
- **Daily garbage/recycling by 5pm.** Alternating between Sean and Liam.
- **Impromptu responsibilities every day.** Help when asked, no pushback.

### Timeline

| Phase | Dates | What happens |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Baseline | Nov 13-20 | Boys with us. Establish expectations, collect baseline data. |
| Dad's week | Nov 20-27 | Boys at their dad's. No tracking. |
| Trial starts | Nov 27 | Boys return. 30-day trial begins. |
| Trial review | ~Dec 27 | Review tracker data. Adjust before birthdays/Christmas. |

> **Note:** The 30-day trial spans ~4.5 calendar weeks, but boys are only with us ~16 days (custody alternation). Tracker data has gaps during dad's weeks. Consistent patterns across tracked weeks are meaningful even with gaps.
