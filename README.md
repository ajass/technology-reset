# Technology Reset — Floor Expectations

**Aaron & Denise | November 2026**

## Executive Summary

Sean and Liam are 11-12 years old. We've been managing their screen time closely, and it's not working anymore — they spend their screen-free time waiting to get back on devices, and we spend our energy enforcing restrictions. This is a structured approach to shifting from **managing their screen time** to **helping them manage their own time**, within a clear responsibility floor.

The plan runs in two phases. Phase 1 establishes the floor with parent enforcement and collects baseline data. Phase 2 grants screen autonomy and tests whether they can handle it, with the data driving rollback decisions.

---

## The Two Phases

This is the core of the plan. The floor (homework, chores, family participation) is the same in both phases. What changes is who enforces it and when.

### Phase 1 — Baseline (Nov 13-20)

**What happens:** The floor is established and parent-enforced. Parents pre-check each floor item before screens open. No autonomy yet — this is the same kind of structure the boys are used to, but with clearer expectations and a tracker running alongside it.

**How it works:**
- Parent checks homework before screens: "Did you finish? Show me."
- Parent checks chores before screens: "Is your laundry done? Did you vacuum?"
- Parent enforces all transitions (10am, 9pm screens off, bedtime)
- Tracker data collected daily — Green/Yellow/Red on four categories

**Purpose:** Teach the floor, collect baseline data, and assess readiness. Are they close to meeting the floor on their own? Can they handle it without much steering? The baseline data answers that question before autonomy is granted.

### Phase 2 — 30-Day Trial (Nov 27 - Dec 27)

**What happens:** The floor is already established (from Phase 1). Boys get screen autonomy — they self-manage their free time. Parents step back from pre-checking and instead steer on failure.

**How it works:**
- Boys self-manage: they do homework, chores, and transitions on their own
- If a parent finds them on screens with the floor unmet, they redirect: "Get it done, then screens are back." Comply = Yellow. Refuse = Red.
- No pre-check gate — the floor is their responsibility to meet
- Tracker data continues daily

**Rollback:** If the data shows 3+ Reds in a week, autonomy isn't working yet. We roll back to Phase 1-style parent-checked structure for 2 weeks, then re-test. There is always a path back to autonomy.

**Purpose:** Test whether they can handle autonomy. The data does the talking at the 30-day review — not gut feeling.

### What Stays the Same in Both Phases

The floor itself doesn't change. These are non-negotiable in Phase 1 and Phase 2:
- No recreational screens before school
- Screens off means ALL screens (gaming, YouTube, TV, reels, phones, iPads)
- Internet safety protections
- Bedtime and screen-off times
- Impromptu asks: help when asked, no pushback
- Family meals (with flexibility — see open items)

### What's Different at a Glance

| | Phase 1 — Baseline | Phase 2 — Trial |
|---|---|---|
| **Who checks the floor** | Parent pre-checks before screens | Boy self-manages, parent steers on failure |
| **Screen access** | Granted by parent after floor verified | Self-granted — autonomy within the floor |
| **Parent role** | Enforcer — verifies and gates | Observer — redirects when floor unmet |
| **Tracker purpose** | Establish baseline behavior | Test autonomy, drive rollback decisions |
| **Duration** | 1 week (Nov 13-20) | 30 days (Nov 27 - Dec 27) |
| **If floor isn't met** | Parent keeps screens closed until done | Parent redirects, logs Yellow/Red |

---

## Key Decisions

- **Homework first, not time-based.** Responsibilities before screens, no clock to fight over.
- **Garbage and recycling: part of the floor.** Done after dishes (week) / as needed (other days). Not a screen gate.
- **Screens off at 9pm = ALL screens** (including reels). No co-watching exceptions.
- **Game-by-game evaluation.** Not platform bans — we approve individual titles.
- **Weekly chores on Saturday.** Sunday is a spot check.
- **Friday and Saturday are their own days.** School days are Monday-Thursday.
- **Impromptu responsibilities every day.** Help when asked, no pushback.
