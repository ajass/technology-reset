## 2026-11-13 (Session 3)

### Changed — Homework Quality Stays Parent-Checked in Both Phases

Homework (including instrument practice) is the one floor item that doesn't go to full autonomy in Phase 2. Boys do homework on their own timeline (when, how — that's the autonomy piece), but parent verifies quality before screens: "Show me what you did." Rushed or sloppy work doesn't count as done — "Fix it, then screens" = Yellow.

Chores, bedtime, screen transitions remain binary and go to full self-management in Phase 2. Homework quality is not binary — a 12-year-old can't reliably self-assess quality.

### Added — What Counts as Homework

Defined "homework" explicitly so boys can't argue loopholes ("not due yet," "just practice"). Definition: anything you're required to do at home that's educational or practice in nature. The due date doesn't matter.

Includes: school assignments (even long-term not due yet),, instrument practice, studying for tests (when scheduled),, online school work (Google Classroom, IXL),, other required educational/practice work (left open for future).

Not homework: chores (separate floor item),, reading (after screens off, before lights out),, optional/enrichment activities.

Added to: floor-expectations (new section),, README (Key Decisions),, fridge-sheet (new callout section),, kid-presentation (fridge rules list),, day-types 01/04/05 (Before Screens references).

Floor items done before screens come back. They can do chores/floor items earlier than 10am if they want, but the expectation is they get moving at 10am.

### Core Distinction Made Explicit Throughout

| | Phase 1 — Baseline | Phase 2 — Trial |
|---|---|---|
| Floor check | Parent pre-checks before screens | Boy self-manages, parent steers on failure |
